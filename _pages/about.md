Since August 2026, I have been an **R&D Engineer** at **Morphi Robot**, working closely with [Tai Wang](https://taiwang.me/). I received my PhD from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in July 2026, where I was a member of the [APRIL Lab](https://april.zju.edu.cn/) under the guidance of [Yong Liu](https://person.zju.edu.cn/yongliu).

From March 2025 to March 2026, I was a visiting researcher at the Department of Computer Science, University of California, Los Angeles, advised by [Bolei Zhou](https://boleizhou.github.io/). From 2023 to 2025, I was a research intern in [ADLab](https://pjlab-adg.github.io/) at [Shanghai AI Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn/). Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from Jianxing Campus of Zhejiang University of Technology in 2021, advised by [Li Yu](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=CnBn6FwAAAAJ).

My research focuses on <strong class="research-highlight">general-purpose embodied intelligence</strong>, with the goal of <strong class="research-highlight">bringing robots into homes everywhere</strong> and freeing people from repetitive and labor-intensive work. You can find my publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=D_GzGFYAAAAJ).
